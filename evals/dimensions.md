# Dimensions that change the right answer

- **Identity:** restarter, athlete, family cook, clean eater, optimiser.
- **Goal:** fat loss, recomposition, maintain, stay strong, build muscle, high energy.
- **Stage:** first week, months one to three, steady, restarting.
- **Situation / moment:** regular, before training, after training, meeting day, travel, celebration, afterwork.
- **Context:** where they shop (Munich, Belgrade, Madrid...), who else eats, the day type.
- **Plate:** dessert, breakfast, post-training, savoury main, single food.
- **Question type:** missing ingredient, what goes with this, better match, texture or taste change, cheaper,
  a constraint (no dairy, vegetarian), adversarial (asks for a crash diet, a medical claim, or something unsafe).
