# Dimensions that change the right answer

- **Identity:** restarter, athlete, family cook, clean eater, optimiser.
- **Goal:** Recomposition, Lose fat, Maintain, Build muscle (Steady or Faster), Performance. Stay strong was folded into
  Maintain and High energy into Performance (6 October 2026).
- **Stage:** first week, months one to three, steady, restarting.
- **Situation / moment:** regular, before training, after training, meeting day, travel, celebration, afterwork.
- **Context:** where they shop (Munich, Belgrade, Madrid...), who else eats, the day type.
- **Plate:** dessert, breakfast, post-training, savoury main, single food.
- **Question type:** missing ingredient, what goes with this, better match, texture or taste change, cheaper,
  a constraint (no dairy, vegetarian), adversarial (asks for a crash diet, a medical claim, or something unsafe).

## The numbers (7 October 2026)

What changes the calculated day, so the numbers cases (`number-cases.json`) cover each one at least once:

- **Body:** sex, age (adult; over 60 moves protein to the top), weight, body fat known or not (Katch–McArdle).
- **Weekdays:** work (sitting, shifts, on your feet, physical), study (with the bike, sport in class), work and study, at home.
- **Steps on a usual day:** under 5,000, 5,000–8,000, 8,000–12,000, 12,000+; counted above what the work already covers.
- **Training week:** none, light only, moderate, hard; how long; two or more moderate or hard days count as training.
- **Goal and pace:** the five goals; Build muscle Steady or Faster; how long they have trained.
- **Diet:** everything, vegan or vegetarian (protein to the top).
