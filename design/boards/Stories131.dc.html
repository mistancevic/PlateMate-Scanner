THE FAT STORE
The fat you eat is fuel and building material; the fat you carry is the store; the one does not become the other by itself. Five words placed: in circulation (Frayn 2010), burned, most in easy work (Venables 2005), used over the day, the honest number (Hall 2012), stored, from any source, fat with the least work (Hellerstein 1999), ketones, normal in a fast or a low-carb day and not fat loss by itself (Cahill 2006).
GLYCOGEN
Glucose moves in the blood, held in a narrow range; glycogen is the store, about 400 g in muscle and 100 g in the liver (Jensen 2011). The liver's store keeps glucose steady and runs low overnight (Nilsson and Hultman 1973); muscle's is used by work and refilled by carbs after (Burke 2011). Only what was used gets refilled; a full store takes nothing more. Your case places the day's carbs after the hard session.
FAT BURNING: WHAT IS TRUE WHEN
A situation page. A walk before breakfast burns more fat during it and the same over the weeks (Schoenfeld 2014). A long easy walk burns most per minute. A hard session runs on carbs, fat after. After a big meal, storage for hours is what a meal is for. A low-carb day brings ketones, not fat loss by itself. A cut below the range drops muscle with the fat. Your case at the week: the trend 0.2 kg against the goal's 0.25 to 0.5, closed by a walk a day and two dinners, not a harder cut.
