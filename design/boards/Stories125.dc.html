DT177A second phone on the counter: watching, hands free
COACH MILAN, 30 OCTOBER 15:39 AND 19:31
Another phone sitting where it can see what is going on during the meal prep. Same need as cook mode: a second screen paired for watching and hands-free cooking. A second person cooking, or someone remote, are add-ons for later.
PAIRING
In cook mode: Use another screen. A 4-letter code and a QR code. The other phone or tablet opens chefmealan.com/cook, types the code or scans. No sign-in, no install. The code works once and ends after 10 minutes.
WHAT THE KITCHEN SCREEN GETS
The recipe and the steps at the amounts you cook, the step buttons, the timers, the same voice commands. Never your numbers, goal or profile. It forgets everything when the session ends.
IN STEP
Both screens show the same step. Next on either ticks it on both. The session lives in your own space in Firestore, owner-only, and the kitchen screen reads it through the code for as long as the session lasts.
CHEF, LOOK
Say it or tap it: the kitchen screen takes one photo of the pan and sends it with the step; Chef Mealan answers aloud. Nothing is sent unless you ask, and the photo is not kept. About 2 cents a session at 5 looks, calculated from Google's paid prices. The notice gets one line.
YOUR PHONE MEANWHILE
Stays in cook mode and says the kitchen screen is on. Take it to the scale or a call; the counter keeps the step. End stops the other screen; Done on either ends the session.
NOT IN DT177
Live video and a voice that speaks up unasked (at least 30 cents a session, and most often wrong on moving food), a second person cooking with their own ticks, cooking along remotely.
