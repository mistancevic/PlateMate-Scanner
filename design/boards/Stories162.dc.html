DT214The cook: one cooking, from start to how it carried you
COACH MILAN, 12:17 TO 12:30
Cooking gets interrupted: something new to scan, a food to add. Disconnect the Plate from meal prep. Add the question about 30 minutes later as the last step. Once disconnected, it can be left and continued at any point until the full cycle is done, with all its photos, later videos, and feedback collected.
TWO THINGS, SIDE BY SIDE
The Plate is what you eat now: craft, fit, log; it is always free. A cook is one time you make something: Make, Eat, How it carried you, with everything said and taken on the way. One cook runs at a time; others wait as saved for later.
A COOK AND ITS RECIPE
A recipe is the instructions, used many times. A cook is one time you made it, and points to its recipe, which it never changes. Started from the Plate with no recipe, the cook carries a draft; at the end Chef Mealan asks: keep it as a new recipe, update yours, or only this time.
KEPT, AND CONTINUED
A cook is saved on the phone and in your account from its first tap. Leave it at any step; the strip on every tab says where it stands and Go on brings you back to the same place. It is the meal card Today already keeps, only started at Start cooking instead of after Feedback.
THE CYCLE
Make: the steps with their photos, the final photo, How did the cooking go? Eat: How much did you eat? How did it taste? (a batch: how many, the rest made, 3 left). Then, about 30 minutes on, How did it carry you? The answer closes the cook. A meal with no cooking skips Make: Eat, then How it carried you.
IN THE MIDDLE OF COOKING
The Plate is free. A food scanned asks: use it in the dish you are cooking, or keep it in your foods. Use it: Chef Mealan asks what it replaces or adds, and the steps follow.
A RECIPE COLLECTS ITS COOKS
Its cooks' photos, how often it went as the steps said, how it tasted, how it carried you. The best photo of a step can become the step's photo.
