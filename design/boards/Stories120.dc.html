DT168Data privacy: the rules keep the promises, not only the screens
COACH MILAN, 30 OCTOBER 10:57
We need to fix data privacy. Double check that, and if other parts also need it.
HOW IT WAS CHECKED
Read in the code, nothing run: the database rules, every server route, file storage, what goes to Gemini and other services, the phone's own storage, sign-out, export and delete. A second, independent read of the server and storage. Not tested on live data.
HIGH
1. A coach's account can read a client's whole record, by the rules: body data, health answers, allergies, the library, notes, own words. The app shows only part of it. 2. Sign-out leaves everything on the phone; the next person to sign in on that phone gets it saved into their account. 3. A coach can change any field of a client's record, also allergies and the 18+ date.
MEDIUM
4. Photo copies from before October are readable by the coach. 5. Any coach account can read access requests and book sign-ups (name, email, phone). 6. Delete my account leaves published recipes, cook status and some access requests. 7. Export my data is incomplete (photo files, recipes). 8. Gemini gets the ticked health situations and allergies, and the notice doesn't say so. 9. The members' food catalogue keeps each food's market and date: in a small pilot that can point to a person.
LOW
10. A coach's send-back note shows on the public recipe page. 11. Member numbers use a salt that can be guessed. 12. Open Food Facts search and the coach's Airtable button aren't in the notice. 13. A food name and a user id in the server logs; a coach's photo stays in the browser cache an hour.
HOLDS
The coach's photo route checks the link and the share; photo files are owner-only; no route logs what people write; Gemini gets no name, email or id.
THE FIX
The record splits in two: a private part only the person reads, and the part the coach needs. The server reads the private part only for safety. Coach writes limited to goal, formula, notes, check-in day and the chat confirmation. Sign-out clears the phone, and data is tagged by account so it never passes to another. Old photo copies owner-only, then deleted. Requests and sign-ups owner-only. Delete and export complete. Public recipes stripped. Logs cleaned. The notice says what goes to Gemini and Open Food Facts. Existing accounts move over on their next save; nothing is lost.
YOUR CHOICES
Members' catalogue: drop market and date, or opt-in. Weight to the coach: trend as direction and % unless body data is shared, or kg as today and the notice says so. Airtable button: remove, or list Airtable. A secret for member numbers: one line in the deploy settings, the step written for you.
