DT174Cook mode: two doors into Make, one big step at a time
COACH MILAN, 30 OCTOBER 16:05 TO 16:31
DT174, draw. Start cooking and Make it may conflict. The Plate is the editor; the recipe is where we read and decide to make it. Two paths: straight from the recipe when it is exactly how we want to cook it, or through the Plate for a little adjustment first. Both land on the Make step.
TWO DOORS ON THE RECIPE
Cook it as written: straight to Make, with the recipe's amounts, nothing to edit. Change it first: today's Make it, the Plate to edit, then Lock the recipe and Make. Edit recipe stays as it is.
START COOKING, ON MAKE
Make already lists the steps to tick. Start cooking sits above them and opens cook mode, full screen: one big step at a time, read aloud by the phone's own voice, big Back, Repeat and Next. Each finished step is ticked on Make; close any time and the ticks stay. The screen stays on.
WHAT YOU CAN SAY
Next, back, repeat. How much rice: the amount you cook, read aloud. Timer 6 minutes: a timer named after its step; several run at once, and the phone rings and says which one ended. What's left. Stop.
THE END
Done goes back to Make with every step ticked. A photo, how it went, how much you ate, Rate it: as today.
FREE TO RUN
The browser's own speech recognition turns the voice into words, and the phone's own voice reads. Nothing goes to Gemini in DT174, so it costs us nothing. Questions to Chef Mealan while cooking come in DT175.
PRIVACY
Voice is asked for once, with where it goes: Google in Chrome, Apple in Safari. No recordings kept. The privacy notice gets one line. Buttons only works the same, and is kept as your answer.
LIMITS WE TEST
Browsers stop listening after a silence, so the app starts listening again. Firefox has no speech recognition: buttons only there. iPhones listen less reliably than Android. Commands are in English, as the app is. A walkthrough with a stand-in voice checks every command; then Testers 1, 2 and 3 cook one recipe each on their own phones.
