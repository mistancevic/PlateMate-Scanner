DT174Cook mode: one big step at a time, by voice or by tap
COACH MILAN, 30 OCTOBER 15:39 AND 16:05
Voice support to make recipes, on the same phone first. Yes, let's start with new DTs. DT174, draw.
WHAT IT DOES
Start cooking opens the steps one at a time, in big type, read aloud by the phone's own voice. Back, Repeat and Next are big buttons for wet hands. The bar on top shows where you are. The screen stays on until you close it.
WHAT YOU CAN SAY
Next, back, repeat. How much rice: the amount from the recipe, read aloud. Timer 6 minutes: a timer named after its step; several run at once, and the phone rings and says which one ended. What's left: how many steps, and the next one. Stop: leaves cook mode.
WHERE
On every recipe with steps, under Steps, with the amounts on the page: when Cook it makes a bigger batch, the steps say the bigger amounts. On Plate, under the plate's steps, with the plate's amounts. The end goes back where you started.
FREE TO RUN
The browser's own speech recognition turns the voice into words, and the phone's own voice reads. Nothing goes to Gemini in DT174, so it costs us nothing. Questions to Chef Mealan while cooking come in DT175.
PRIVACY
Voice is asked for once, with where it goes: Google in Chrome, Apple in Safari. No recordings kept. The privacy notice gets one line. Buttons only works the same, and is kept as your answer.
LIMITS WE TEST
Browsers stop listening after a silence, so the app starts listening again. Firefox has no speech recognition: buttons only there. iPhones listen less reliably than Android. Commands are in English, as the app is. A walkthrough with a stand-in voice checks every command; then Testers 1, 2 and 3 cook one recipe each on their own phones.
