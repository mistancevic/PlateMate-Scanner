NOW, AFTER DT158 (DRAWN)
Micros starts from a food: one portion and the members it brings. There is no way to start from a vitamin or a mineral.
THE STORY
Micros gets two chips: By food (DT158) and By member. By member lists the twelve in their three groups; a tap opens the member's page. Each page has the same sections, each a heading, a line on what it is for, then the details. WHAT IT IS. WHAT IT DOES: the EU's authorised health claims, word for word. HOW MUCH A DAY: your day from EFSA, the label's reference, the upper limit. WHERE TO FIND IT: one normal portion of each food against your day, the line at the whole day, green when one portion covers it; for vitamin D, the form, D2 or D3. COMBINE IT WITH: what helps your body use it, and why. TOO MUCH. Sources. Food first: supplements appear only in the combine line and the upper limit. Vitamin D and Vitamin C first; the other ten follow the same page.
WHAT THE NUMBERS SHOW
Vitamin D: 100 g of UV-exposed mushrooms bring 175 % of your 15 µg, 150 g of salmon 131 %, a can of sardines 29 %, a glass of milk 19 %, two eggs 13 %. Vitamin C: a red pepper brings 155 % of your 110 mg; broccoli 81 %, strawberries 80 %, a kiwi and an orange 63 % each.
THE NUMBERS, CHECKED
Foods: USDA rows with their FDC ids, SR Legacy, milk and red pepper the Foundation rows. EFSA: vitamin D 15 µg for adults (adequate intake) and an upper limit of 100 µg; vitamin C 110 mg for men and 95 mg for women, no upper limit set. Label references: Regulation (EU) No 1169/2011, Annex XIII: D 5 µg, C 80 mg. Claims: Regulation (EU) No 432/2012, Annex; the vitamin C rows from the legislation.gov.uk copy, the vitamin D rows from a copy of the register; to read on EUR-Lex before the build. D3 and D2: Tripkovic 2012, Am J Clin Nutr, a review of trials. Vitamin K: Kuang 2020 (a review, bone density) and Witham 2020 (a trial, people with kidney disease); not settled, and the EU has no claim. Iron over months: Nyakundi 2026, Am J Clin Nutr.
