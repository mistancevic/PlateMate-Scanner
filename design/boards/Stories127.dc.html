DT179From your calendar to the app
FROM THE PLAN OF 30 OCTOBER
The second calendar part. DT178 sends your plan out to Google Calendar. DT179 reads two things back: when you are busy, and events of Chef Mealan's own calendar you moved there.
THE SWITCH
Google Calendar page: Read when I'm busy, off at first. On asks Google for one more right, calendar.freebusy: busy and free times only. Never titles, people or places.
WHAT PLAN DOES WITH BUSY TIMES
A meal in a meeting: before, after, or keep. A late evening: a quick dinner, or cook the day before. A full day: a lunch to carry. Training in a busy block: move it, a rest day, or keep it.
SUGGEST, NEVER OVERWRITE
Your times change only when you tap. A tap moves the meal for that date, as a hand-set time does, with Back to 13:15. The calendar is one more setting after work and study.
MOVED IN GOOGLE COMES BACK
Drag Friday's dinner from 20:00 to 21:00 in Google Calendar: Chef Mealan reads its own calendar and Friday's dinner is at 21:00 here, with Back to 20:00.
WHERE IT RUNS
On your phone, when the app opens and on Plan, with the same hour as DT178: after it, one tap on Update Google Calendar reads and writes. Busy times are not stored on our server, not shared with your coach, not sent to the AI.
WHAT YOU DO
Add the second right to the sign-in screen settings and to the review. Nothing else.
NOT IN DT179
Reading the titles of your events, such as a gym class. Apple and Outlook calendars.
