THE STORY
The goal setup gets a step, 3. Carbs and fat, after the protein card. Four chips: Balanced (fat 30 %, today's rule, the default), More carbs (25 %), More fat (35 %), Very low carb (you type your carbs; fat takes the rest). The Performance goal starts on More carbs, as its 25 % is today. A card like the protein card shows your fat in the 20 to 35 % range and what carbs get, and names the days carbs sit below 45 %. Very low carb says where it sits against both ranges, and never tells you to change. The day table follows the chip. Protein stays set by the goal. We ask how you like to eat, not what you believe: the app records a preference, never a health claim. What you eat gets Paleo (no grains, legumes or dairy), Meat only (meat, fish and eggs; it says carbs come close to zero and links to the Carbs and fat step) and Other, a line in your words. Chef Mealan reads what you eat, the Other line and the Carbs and fat chip with every plate and recipe it suggests. The Explorer's Calculated for you line names the chip.
WHAT THE NUMBERS SHOW
The same training profile as DT160 (week average 2,500 kcal, protein 160 g). Balanced: fat 83 g, carbs 228 to 366 g. More carbs: fat 69 g, carbs 260 to 397 g. More fat: fat 97 g, carbs 197 to 334 g, below 45 % on every day but Monday. Very low carb with 50 g typed: fat 162 to 223 g, 63 to 70 % of the energy.
THE NUMBERS, CHECKED
25, 30 and 35 % all sit inside the 20 to 35 % fat range (EFSA 2010; AND, DC and ACSM 2016). Very low carb has no number of ours: the person types it. Every day number is the app's own calculation for this profile, with the chip's fat share.
