The cycle, per month, for everyone who has one: appetite in the second half (Davidsen 2007, a few hundred kcal a day), water and the scale, temperature (Baker and Driver 2007), training (McNulty 2020, 78 studies: a trivial difference), iron (EFSA 2015, the Micros page), enough food (Loucks and Thuma 2003). The app does not ask or store the day of the cycle; the case reads weigh-ins and plates only.
THYROID, PER MONTH, ONE SCREEN
A long deep cut turns the rate down (Rosenbaum and Leibel 2010; Loucks 2003 measured T3 too): cold hands and tiredness on a deep cut, the fix is the range. Iodine 150 µg and selenium 70 µg from food (EFSA 2014); both join Micros in the same build. Sleep. No test, no level, no booster.
GROWTH HORMONE, PER DAY, ONE SCREEN
The first deep sleep of the night (Van Cauter, Leproult and Plat 2000), a hard session (Kraemer and Ratamess 2005), fasting (Ho 1988), and what the claims get wrong: boosters do nothing lasting in healthy people.
THE WELLNESS LINE
The cycle has the strictest line after The brain: no day asked, no condition, no pain advice, no test, no supplement; a cycle that changes is for your doctor. Thyroid and growth hormone: never a level or a treatment.
NOT IN THIS BOARD
Cycle tracking. Thyroid conditions, medication. Growth hormone as a treatment.
