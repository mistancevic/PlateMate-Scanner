THEORY, WITH SOURCES

What causes it: coffee after 14:00 (Drake 2013), a drink in the evening (Ebrahim 2013), a late heavy dinner (Crispim 2011), a hard session late (Stutz 2019). The loop. Your numbers do not change.

TREND AGAINST TRAJECTORY

HORIZONS

THE WELLNESS LINE
