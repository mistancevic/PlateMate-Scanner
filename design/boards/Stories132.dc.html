DT183The body: Muscle
COACH MILAN, 31 OCTOBER 13:00 GO
The first of five family boards for The body, three screens each, the shape of DT182. Muscle: built and taken apart all day; protein and strength training tip it one way, doing without tips it the other.
WHAT MOVES IT
Protein per meal, about 0.4 g per kg, about 30 g, four such meals a day (Schoenfeld and Aragon 2018; Moore 2009). Leucine, about 2.5 g in a meal starts the building (Devries 2018). Strength training, sets close to hard, two or more times a week per muscle (Schoenfeld 2017). Doing without: two weeks lose measurable muscle (Wall 2013). Age: from about 50 the same protein builds less, and the protein range on Goal already rises with the year of birth (Bauer 2013, PROT-AGE).
WHEN IT HELPS, WHEN IT HURTS
The day after a session, not only the first hour. Protein before bed. A fat loss phase with protein at the top of the range and the strength days kept. A rest day, protein stays. Two weeks of nothing: walking and protein keep most of it.
YOUR CASE, PER MONTH
Protein on the 7-day average against the range, per meal from the plates; the strength days done; the weigh-ins. The gap is the one meal below 30 g, breakfast.
THE WELLNESS LINE
No body composition reading, no supplement beyond food, no booster. Whey and skyr are foods on the Protein page.
SOURCES TO CHECK BEFORE A BUILD
Schoenfeld and Aragon 2018; Moore 2009; Devries 2018; Schoenfeld, Ogborn and Krieger 2017; Wall 2013; Bauer 2013.
