DT178Chef Mealan's own calendar in Google
FROM THE PLAN OF 30 OCTOBER
The calendar part, from the app to the calendar. Your meals, cooking, shopping and training show where you live your day, next to your meetings. Reading busy times is DT179.
WHERE
Me, under Training week: Google Calendar. Off until you turn it on. Connect asks Google for one right only: create the Chef Mealan calendar and manage its events (calendar.app.created). Never your other events.
WHAT GOES IN
Meals, Cooking, Training, Shopping: on at first. Weigh in and Check-in: off at first, because calendars get shared. The dish's name: on, and you can turn it off. 14 days ahead.
WHAT AN EVENT SAYS
The name, the time, and a link that opens that day on Plan. Never kcal, protein, your goal or health words.
WHEN IT UPDATES
While the app is open: a change on Plan shows in Google within seconds. When you open the app, the next 14 days are brought up to date. No Google key is kept on our server, so nothing changes while the app is closed.
SHOPPING
A kept shopping list can get a day: Shop on, on Plan. No day, no event.
TURN OFF
Remove the Chef Mealan calendar, or keep it as it is. Deleting the account removes it. The privacy notice gets these lines.
BEFORE GOOGLE'S REVIEW
Up to 100 people can connect, counted for the life of the project. They see Google's screen that says the app is not verified yet. Google lists about 10 business days for the review.
WHAT YOU DO, ONCE, WHEN WE BUILD
Turn on the Google Calendar API, add the one right to the sign-in screen settings, submit the review. One step at a time; I send each as one screen.
NOT IN DT178
Busy times and events moved in Google coming back (DT179). Apple and Outlook calendars.
