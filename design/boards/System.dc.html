Chef Mealan: the system, and who does what on every path
As of 0.3.50. Top: the parts and what runs where. Below: the 18 happy paths step by step, each step tagged with what does it. Code is deterministic and tested by unit tests and walkthroughs. Rules are the coach's rules written as code, tested the same way and by the rule evals. LLM is a Gemini call, tested by evals with a human label and a judge. Human is red, by role: Client, Coach, Owner. A human step that says checks, corrects or approves is a human in the loop: a person checking what code or the model produced before it counts.

THE PARTS, IN WORDS
People
A person on the phone. A coach (Milan) on the same app with a sixth tab. The owner (Milan) on Metrics, Running costs, Evals.
Testers 1 to 3 on preview. Claude builds: brainstorm, Draw, Go; tests; releases.
Not yet: a second developer, a reviewer for German, a designer. The boards, the rules and this map are what they would read first.
→uses
The phone: chefmealan.com
A web app (React, TypeScript, Vite) installed from the browser. Works offline for the plate and the numbers.
Everything about the person lives on the phone first: profile, goal, foods, plates, recipes, the week. Kept in localStorage, sent to the account a second after changes settle.
Camera: labels, barcodes, Photo of my meal. Speech: the browser's own, for Say what you have and cooking (no AI, nothing sent).
The calculator (PD, the letters, the fit, the plan): code on the phone, no server needed.
Public pages: home, /recipes, /r/slug, /built, legal.
→HTTPS, JSON
The server: Cloud Run, Frankfurt
Express, one process, 70 routes. Serves the app and the public pages.
Every AI call goes through here; the phone never holds a key.
Rules in code: the coach's rules (docs/rules) on plates, drinks, meals a day, the eating window, what the app may say.
Counting: events, visits, the AI bill per call; Metrics and Running costs for the owner.
The kitchen screen: a 4-letter code, two screens read the same session every 2 s.
Deploy: ./deploy/preview.sh, then promote; one-line rollback; the version at the foot of every page.
→key on the server only
Gemini (Google)
gemini-3.8-flash first, 3.5-flash-lite and 3.6-flash as the fallbacks; the fastest answer wins.
What asks the model: Photo of my meal (foods, grams), Scan a label (the table), Chef Mealan's questions and chat, Chef Mealan's checks on a plate, a recipe from a screenshot or a link, the steps, a swap, the tip, the cover picture, the judge in Evals.
Every answer must fit a schema; a bad one is dropped, the fallback asked.
Price per call counted; target under €1 per active member a month.
Firebase (Google)
Auth: sign in with Google. Firestore: the account's copy of the phone's data, coach and client links, shared cards, invites, waiting list, metrics.
Storage: photos as files, Frankfurt. Access and deletion by the person: export, full delete.
Also
Open Food Facts: a barcode or a search, the product's name and picture.
Google Calendar: training and busy times, when switched on.
GitHub: the code, design/boards, docs/rules, docs/qa/runs. Public today.
How we know it works
Unit tests 531, every push. Walkthroughs 148 in a real browser, every release, the run kept in docs/qa/runs. Foundations: colours, sizes, 44 px.
Evals 90 cases in 7 sets: numbers and swaps (code, every push); photo advice (rules); the chat, the plate questions, the photo read (model, before a release, server and key needed); the judge and its agreement with the coach.
Missing: model evals on every push; a trace per AI call (prompt, answer, cost) to read when a case fails.
