Then your case in the five parts at the member's horizon, with trend against trajectory. The chat chips. Nothing changes unless you tap.
GLUCOSE AND INSULIN
The curve after a meal. Helps: the morning with protein and fibre; before and after training; a carb dinner after a hard day for the body, it refills the muscle glycogen that was used. Watch: a hard day for the head, nothing to refill, the carbs count like any others; after a short night. Hurts: a quiet day's late evening. What is refilled is glycogen, never glucose, and only what was used; a heavy meal helps nothing. The evidence on carbs at night is mixed, and the page says so: a heavy late meal hurts sleep (Crispim 2011), carbs four hours before bed helped sleep onset (Afaghi 2007). Never no carbs at night.
YOUR CASE AT THE MEAL AND THE DAY
Reads the Plate's letters P, C and Fi, the GI from our Carbs table, the training week and the kind of day. On a hard training day the tap is the bigger carb meal after the session; on a quiet Monday, fibre on the canteen plate.
TESTOSTERONE, HUNGER HORMONES, CORTISOL
The four levers with their papers, then helps and hurts: the hour after a session is normal, chasing it is not; hunger before a planned meal is right, hunger at 22:30 after a short night is the loop, and the case points to the situation page. Cortisol: right in the morning, wrong in the evening.
THE WELLNESS LINE
What happens in anyone. The page explains the curve; it does not read your numbers, does not say what a number means for you, names no condition, no treatment, no supplement, no booster. Someone told to watch their glucose follows their doctor's plan; the app follows the numbers given to it.
