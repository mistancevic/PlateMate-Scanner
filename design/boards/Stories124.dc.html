DT176Travel mode: a manual switch, the app serves where you are
COACH MILAN, 30 OCTOBER 19:27 AND 19:31
Travel mode, activated manually: the person is travelling, and the app temporarily serves that place. Manual switch, no end date.
THE SWITCH
On Me, above the steps. On when you leave, off when you are back. Your profile does not change; travel sits on top of it, and Turn off puts everything back as it was.
TURNING IT ON ASKS TWO THINGS
Where you are: one of your places from Where I shop and cook, or Elsewhere. What you have there: a hotel with no kitchen (nothing to cook, buy and order), a flat with a kitchen (stovetop, pan, pot; tick more), or family and friends (a full kitchen, their shops).
WHILE IT IS ON
Every tab shows the travel banner with Turn off. Scan and search find products sold in that country. Chef Mealan suggests what fits the kitchen you have: in a hotel, what to buy and what to order, and how a plate of it fits your numbers; Start cooking is hidden. Weigh-in pauses: a hotel scale is a different scale, and your trend keeps home mornings only. Training week and your numbers stay as they are.
OFF
Shops, kitchen, tools and weigh-in come back. Nothing was rewritten. The meals of the trip stay in your week, as every meal does, and the days are marked as travel for you and, if the day part is shared, for your coach.
WHAT IT COSTS
Nothing new: the same calls as today. The country only changes which shelves the search reads.
NOT IN DT176
An end date, a calendar link, or guessing from the phone's location. You switch it on and off.
