DT175Ask Chef while cooking; say what you have
COACH MILAN, 30 OCTOBER 15:39 AND 17:36
Voice support to make recipes according to the ingredients and the process of preparation. In the meantime, draw the next DT.
ASK CHEF, IN COOK MODE
Start with Chef, and the question goes to Chef Mealan: Chef, I only have 120 g chicken, what do I change? With it go the recipe, this step, the amounts you cook, your kitchen and tools, your allergies and ticked situations, as with every question to Chef Mealan; never your name or email. The answer shows under the step and is read aloud.
WITHOUT CHEF, NOTHING LEAVES
The seven commands stay on the phone, as in 0.3.16. Talk in the kitchen without Chef first is not sent anywhere. With voice off, the same question can be typed under the step.
IT NEVER CHANGES THE PLATE
An answer is advice. The amounts, the numbers and the steps stay as they are; Change it first is still where the plate changes.
SAY WHAT YOU HAVE, ON THE PLATE
A new button under Add a food. Say the foods in any order, with amounts if you know them. Foods you have are taken from your foods; the rest come with typical values, marked typical until you scan the pack. Then Fit to my target: Chef Mealan builds the dinner around them and writes the steps, as today.
WHAT IT COSTS
One question or one list of foods is one call to Gemini 3.8 Flash. With about 3,000 tokens in and 200 out, that is $0.0023 + $0.0008, about 0.3 cents. From 1 January 2027 the price doubles: about 0.6 cents. Calculated from Google's paid prices.
WHEN CHEF CAN'T ANSWER
No connection, or the AI is off: Chef can't answer right now; the steps, the commands and the timers still work.
LIMITS WE TEST
Chef Mealan's answers here are stand-ins written for the board. A walkthrough with stand-in answers checks the path; Testers 1, 2 and 3 ask real questions while cooking.
