Wake-up and Bedtime are asked under Recovery and sent to Chef Mealan, but they move no meal. Without an eating window or fixed work hours, the meals sit at 07:30 to 20:00 for everyone, evenly between. Someone up before 6:00 and in bed before 22:00 gets breakfast at 07:30 and dinner at 20:00.
THE STORY
Wake-up sets the first meal: an hour and a half after the start of your wake-up range. Bedtime sets the last: two hours before the start of your bedtime range. The meals between sit evenly, as now. Before 6:00 counts as 5:00, before 22:00 as 21:00. So: before 6:00 gives 06:30, 6:00 to 8:00 gives 07:30, after 8:00 gives 09:30; before 22:00 gives 19:00, 22:00 to midnight gives 20:00, after midnight gives 22:00. The middle answers give today's 07:30 and 20:00, so most people see no change. Varies with shifts: as now.
WHAT STAYS
A time you set by hand always wins. Fixed work hours still come first, as since 0.2.128, with one change: breakfast is never set before you wake (at most as work starts). An eating window set before still wins. One meal a day stays at 18:00. The Meal times setting says where the times come from; Wake-up and Bedtime say what they set.
THE NUMBERS
One and a half hours and two hours are our rule, not a study's: chosen so the middle answers give the times the app has always used. Yours to change, as the coach.
