COACH MILAN, 30 OCTOBER 18:09 TO 19:23
Add the pictures with a Beta label; craft and check one picture at a time. Mind the sizes of buttons and where labels sit over photos. Where can the video go? Make the steps on the blue bar clickable, not only back and forth. Redraw DT175a.
SIZES AND LABELS
Labels sit under a picture, never on it. Nothing smaller than 12 px. Every button 44 px high or more, the size Apple recommends for touch.
STEP BUTTONS
The 5 px bar in cook mode becomes six numbered buttons. Done steps show a tick, the current one is blue. Tap a number, or say go to step 4, and the step shows and is read aloud. Jumping ahead ticks nothing; only Next ticks.
WATCH THE STEPS
On top of the recipe, where you read and decide: the step pictures play one after another, about 5 seconds each, with each step's words and timer under the picture. The numbers jump; Pause; Cook it as written goes straight to Make. Made on the phone from the pictures: no video file, nothing to pay. Shown only when every step has a picture.
PICTURES FOR STEPS
One per step, small beside it, labelled under it. Added from the camera or the gallery, for example a picture made in ChatGPT or Gemini with the Step Picture Checks prompts. Who made it is asked; the checks show before Keep it. In cook mode the picture sits on top of the step: Picture drawn by AI, Beta, or Your photo.
WHERE THEY LIVE
With the recipe, as image files only you can open, like your other photos. Not shared with members in Beta. Deleted with the recipe or the account; in Export my data.
NOT IN DT175A
Drawing pictures inside the app, an automatic check of a picture, a filmed video. They wait for your real session photos.
