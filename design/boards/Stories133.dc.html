DT184The body: Bone
COACH MILAN, 31 OCTOBER 13:00 GO
Bone is rebuilt all life long, slowly. Calcium and vitamin D are the material, strength and impact the signal, and the hormones for everyone keep the balance.
WHAT MOVES IT
Calcium and vitamin D with EFSA's values by age, already on the Micros pages. Load: lifting, jumping, running, stairs; swimming and cycling do little here (Watson 2018, LIFTMOR). Protein, a third of bone by weight (Rizzoli 2018, ESCEO). Eating too little for too long lowers the protecting hormones in everyone (Loucks 2004). Time: peak around 30 (Weaver 2016).
WHEN IT HELPS, WHEN IT HURTS
A strength day; the meal after with dairy or greens; a week in a hotel, walking and stairs keep the signal; months of a deep cut, the range on Goal is the guard.
YOUR CASE, PER MONTH
Calcium on the 7-day average against the day, from the Micros page; vitamin D in the dark months, EFSA's own note; both strength days. The gap is one calcium portion a day.
THE WELLNESS LINE
No bone density reading, no condition named, no supplement beyond the Micros foods and vitamin D in winter, which EFSA names. For bone health: your doctor.
SOURCES TO CHECK BEFORE A BUILD
EFSA 2015 and 2016 DRVs; Watson 2018; Rizzoli 2018; Loucks 2004; Weaver 2016.
