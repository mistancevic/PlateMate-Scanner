THE STORY
Each mineral opens on its own page, the same sections as the vitamins: what it is, what it does (the EU claims, word for word), how much a day for you, as a range, where to find it, what to combine it with, too much. Where to find it: the ten foods on our table with the most in one normal portion, taken from the shared table, so the numbers are the ones in Foods. Six foods are added to the table, read in full, so they are in Foods too: chickpeas, sardines, pumpkin seeds, tofu set with calcium, kale and beef liver. 30 foods.
WHAT EACH PAGE SAYS
Calcium: 950 mg from 25, 1000 mg at 18 to 24, from the year of birth in your profile. Iron: 11 mg for a man, 16 mg for a woman before menopause and 11 mg after; the app doesn't ask, so the page says both. No upper limit: EFSA set a safe level of intake, 40 mg a day. Magnesium: EFSA's upper limit, 250 mg, is for supplements, water and added magnesium only, and sits below your day, so the range says no upper limit for food, and why. Potassium: 3500 mg; no upper limit, not enough data. Zinc: EFSA's value rises with phytate, 9.4 to 16.3 mg for a man; your day is the lowest, and the page says so.
THE NUMBERS, CHECKED
EU claims, Regulation (EU) No 432/2012: calcium 8, iron 7, magnesium 10, potassium 3, zinc 18, word for word. Label references, Regulation (EU) No 1169/2011: 800, 14, 375, 2000 and 10 mg. EFSA: calcium 2015, iron 2015, magnesium 2015, potassium 2016, zinc 2014; upper limits from EFSA's overview, version 11. The six new USDA SR Legacy rows read on getfoodfacts.com today: chickpeas 173757, sardines 175139, pumpkin seeds 170556, tofu 172475, kale 168421, beef liver 168627. Per 100 g, for example: tofu 683 mg calcium, sardines 382 mg, pumpkin seeds 592 mg magnesium, beef liver 6.17 mg iron.
