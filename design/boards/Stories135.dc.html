DT186The body: The liver
COACH MILAN, 31 OCTOBER 13:00 GO
The liver keeps blood glucose steady between meals, holds the small glycogen store, makes ketones when carbs are low, clears alcohol and handles fructose. The body's chemistry between meals.
WHAT MOVES IT
Between meals and overnight: glucose let out and made new, breakfast refills (Nilsson and Hultman 1973; Jensen 2011). Ketones after a day or more of low carbs (Cahill 2006). Alcohol: about one drink an hour, fat burning stops meanwhile, the second half of the night breaks; counted as energy at 7 kcal a gram (Siler 1999; Ebrahim 2013). Fructose: small in whole fruit, not in drinks (Tappy and Lê 2010). Fat in the liver from energy over the total for a long time, in anyone, and out again with weight loss (Vilar-Gomez 2015).
WHEN IT HELPS, WHEN IT HURTS
Breakfast after a short night. Whole fruit. A low-carb day, ketones by evening, normal. A drink with dinner, counted, water with it. Sweet drinks: the one swap the Carbs page asks for.
YOUR CASE, PER DAY
From the plates, the drinks and the sleep habit: two drinks on Thursday, a short night, Friday's breakfast as the fix.
THE WELLNESS LINE
No liver value, no condition, no detox, no cleanse. For liver health: your doctor.
SOURCES TO CHECK BEFORE A BUILD
Nilsson and Hultman 1973; Jensen 2011; Cahill 2006; Siler 1999; Ebrahim 2013; Tappy and Lê 2010; Vilar-Gomez 2015.
