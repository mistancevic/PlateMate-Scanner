The Explorer shows a meal's amount, from today's numbers, without saying where it comes from. Ranges exist only for protein, on Plan. In the app, protein and fat are the same every day (fat is 30 % of the week's average kcal); carbs take the difference, so they move with the day.
THE STORY
A line at the top of the Explorer: Calculated for you. It names your goal and setup and today's day (a strength day, 2,600 kcal), says the numbers marked for you change with them, and links to Change them. Each family opens with YOUR DAY, marked for you, each range a green band with your number as a mark. Protein: one range, the same every day, 1.6 to 2.2 g per kg when you train, 1.2 to 1.6 when you don't. Fat: 20 to 35 % of your week's average energy, because your fat is set on it and stays the same every day; saturated fat under 10 % of it. Carbs: today's range, 45 to 60 % of today's energy, and under it Your week, from the rest day to your hardest day on the same scale, today marked. When a day falls below 45 %, one orange line says which days and why. Fibre at least 25 g; free sugars under 10 % of today's energy. On a member's page, How much a day becomes a range from your day to the upper limit.
