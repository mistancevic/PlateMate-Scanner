DT216Cooked plates get cooking steps
COACH MILAN, 15:54
Your crêpes: step 2 is everything combined; very hard to work with the voice, and it burns tokens and time.
WHY
The steps were not Chef Mealan's. They are the app's pattern for a bowl you don't cook (put the base in a bowl, add the rest, stir), kept with the recipe when it was saved. The app counts a plate as cooked only when a food's name says raw, dry or uncooked; Eggs and Wheat flour, white don't.
NEW: WHEN A PLATE IS COOKED
Flour, a raw egg, raw meat or fish, or a batter (flour with egg or milk). One culinary rule in one place, with its test.
NEW: ITS STEPS
A cooked plate never gets the bowl pattern. It gets Chef Mealan's steps, 3 to 8, every food in one, at your amounts; or Write my own. A recipe that kept the bowl steps gets the new steps the next time it is made, and Lock the recipe keeps them.
DT217Cooking move by move
COACH MILAN, 16:09
A different process for voice: the recipe stays as it is, cooking goes food by food, step by step.
NEW: MOVES
The recipe keeps its steps as written, to read and decide. Cook mode reads a step one move at a time: one food, or one action. Step 2 of the crêpes as they are now: Add 2 eggs, 104 g. Add 100 g milk. Add 35 g wheat flour. Add 180 g quark. Add 1 banana, 81 g. Add 15 g chocolate chips. Stir until it's one colour.
VERSION 2, 16:35
The whole step stays on top, to follow along. Under it the moves, read aloud one at a time.
ON THE SCREEN
STEP 2 and its whole text. Under it READ ALOUD, ONE AT A TIME · 3 OF 7 and the list of moves: done ones ticked, this one large on blue, the rest plain. The number buttons stay the written steps. Next, Back and Repeat work on moves; go to step 3 jumps to a written step.
TIMERS AND TICKS
A timer belongs to the move that names the minutes. The last move of a step is its action; Next on it ticks the step on Make, as today. A step's photo stays on top through its moves.
WHO MAKES THE MOVES
Chef Mealan drafts them with the steps, once per recipe, kept with it and checked like steps (one food a move, every food in, the amounts from the recipe): a fraction of a cent once, then cooking costs nothing. A recipe without moves gets them in code: each food it names becomes Add, then its own words as the action.
VOICE
One short line at a time: Next, Back and Stop cut in sooner, and a paid voice (VO1) costs less per cook.
